Using the illustrations

Choose an image that supports your point

Scenes work well for problems, processes, and collaboration. Use spot illustrations for sections and states. Choose one main scene per slide and leave room for the title and evidence.

Keep proportions and give it space

Use scenes at 320–720 px wide and spot illustrations at 96–160 px. Scale proportionally and keep people, props, and whitespace intact. Add text separately in your layout.

Transparent PNGs, ready to use

Works well on white, light gray, and light blue. Use a white container on dark slides. Download a PNG for your presentation or copy it from this local preview.
