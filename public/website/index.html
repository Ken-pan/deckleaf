06 / Before you begin

Before you start.

Can I use Decksprig on this website?

Yes. Open Decksprig to create, edit and present immediately. Documents and history stay in this browser, with import and export for portable backups. No account is required.

Who is it for?

Designers preparing portfolios, teams reviewing projects, and anyone shaping a design proposal. Desktop is the primary editing surface. Smaller screens support reading and direct content edits.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Manual editing, templates and presentations work without AI. If you connect a service, review proposals before applying them. Your chosen provider may charge for usage.

How do I use a starter deck?

Download a starter from the Templates section. In Decksprig, open the document menu, choose Import, and select the .deck.json file. Replace the sample content with your own work.

What can I export?

Save an editable Deck JSON file, a PowerPoint presentation, or a standalone HTML presentation. Exported PowerPoint files may need the corresponding fonts on the destination device. Native Keynote export requires Keynote on the local Mac.

How does this relate to Broadser?

Both are tools by Ken Pan. Broadser brings research and sources together on a canvas. Decksprig helps turn your work into a presentation. Each has its own documents and workspace.
