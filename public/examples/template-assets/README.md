# Deckleaf template artwork

All visual assets used inside the six template collections are Deckleaf's own AI-generated media. No third-party photos, illustrations, icon packs, or decorative assets are imported into the template pages.

The 11 PNG illustrations are byte-identical copies from our Blue & Black AI library. Original pixels, transparency, attribution, and SHA-256 values are preserved. The full library and its generation notes remain at `/resources/blue-black-v1/`.

The five photographic studies were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Original PNGs are preserved in `originals/`; the JPEG files are compressed opaque presentation copies. The paper study predates this refinement. Generation prompts for the new coast, material, and glass studies are recorded in `docs/reviews/templates-20261013/refinement/artwork.json` in the source workspace.

These locally generated assets do not claim CC0 or a third-party open license. They are decorative examples, never project evidence. Replace them and their visible captions when presenting actual work.

Fonts remain the existing locally bundled font system with its retained licenses. Rectangles, dividers, editable charts, and tables are native editor objects.

The report paper relief was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Its neutral materials pair with any theme; the cover caption uses the document accent. Original: `originals/report-relief.png`. Generation notes: `docs/reviews/templates-20261013/playback/artwork.json`.

Minimalist has four exclusive studies generated with built-in ImageGen: `minimal-orbit`, `minimal-fold`, `minimal-balance`, and `minimal-void`. Their unmodified PNG originals are in `originals/`, with optimized JPEG copies beside this README. The cover, content/evidence, gallery, and evidence-detail pages use only these images. Other collections do not use this set. Exact prompts and generation hashes are in `docs/reviews/templates-20261013/minimal-artwork/prompts.json` and `artwork.json` in the source workspace.
